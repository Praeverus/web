THE PROBLEM

Jury research is a luxury.
It shouldn’t be.

Traditional jury research is expensive, time-consuming, and typically reserved for the highest-stakes cases. By the time it happens, many of the most important litigation decisions have already been made.

Traditional Jury Research

Rare, late, and expensive.

  • Reserved for a relatively small number of high-stakes cases.
  • Often conducted after significant litigation expenses have already been incurred.
  • Difficult and expensive to repeat as the case evolves.
  • Limited opportunities to test multiple strategies.

With Praeverus

Anytime. Any Stage.

  • Evaluate a case before filing, during discovery, before mediation, or in preparation for trial.
  • Reassess as new evidence, testimony, and rulings emerge.
  • Test alternative arguments and strategies.
  • Make jury intelligence accessible throughout the litigation lifecycle.

TEST YOUR OWN STRATEGY

Don’t Just Build Your Strategy.
Test It.

Compare alternative arguments, evidence presentations, and litigation strategies against a consistent synthetic venue population. Measure whether the proposed change increases, decreases, or leaves unchanged the model’s predicted venue-level response.

  • Baseline and alternative strategy distributions
  • Direction and magnitude of estimated change
  • The same synthetic population in both conditions

A percentage-point movement is a change in the model’s venue-level estimate—a signal to weigh, not proof of how real jurors would be persuaded.

THE SCIENCE BEHIND PRAEVERUS

Built on the Science,
Not Just the Model.

Large language models alone are not designed to reproduce the diversity of human jury decision-making. Praeverus combines AI-based case evaluation with research-informed synthetic populations and structured methods for measuring venue-level responses.

Juror Decision-Making Research

Research into how jurors interpret evidence, develop narratives, form beliefs, and evaluate competing explanations.

Persuasion Theory

Research into how arguments, evidence, credibility, and presentation can influence how information is received.

Behavioral Economics

Research into uncertainty, judgment, cognitive biases, and the ways framing can influence decision-making.

Demographic and Attitudinal Research

Research-informed characteristics used to construct synthetic populations relevant to particular venues.

“The model is only part of the equation. The research-informed methodology around it is what makes Praeverus different.”

Praeverus produces decision-support estimates. They are not guarantees of jury behavior or trial outcomes, and the methodology is not represented as reproducing every aspect of human juror decision-making.
